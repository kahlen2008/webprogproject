Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 · Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

At RecipePlan, we value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website, meal planner, and dietary tools.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only information necessary to deliver personalised culinary recommendations and meal planning services:

Account Information

Your display name, email address, profile picture (or generated avatar), and authentication identifiers provided during sign up or Google OAuth.

Dietary & Health Preferences

Selected dietary preferences (e.g. Vegetarian, Halal, Keto) and allergen avoidances (e.g. Peanuts, Gluten, Dairy) saved during onboarding or in Profile settings.

Meal Plans & Favourites

Recipes saved to your personal favourites collection and weekly meal planner schedules.

Technical Data

Basic browser type, device information, and local storage tokens used to maintain your session state securely.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Personalised Recipe Suggestions: Filtering recipes according to your dietary requirements and allergy warnings.
  • Meal Planner & Grocery Sync: Saving your weekly meal calendar across devices and consolidating pantry shopping ingredients.
  • Account Management: Authenticating your login credentials, managing password resets, and sending essential service notifications.
  • Platform Improvement: Analysing aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage trends to improve performance and recipe variety.

4. Data Storage & Security

RecipePlan uses Google Firebase for authentication and database services (Firestore).

  • Passwords are never stored in plaintext; authentication is processed through Google Firebase Auth's secure cryptographic infrastructure.
  • Browser LocalStorage is utilised solely to cache user session identifiers and improve interface response times.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or dietary profile to any third-party advertisers.

5. Third-Party Integrations

RecipePlan integrates with selected trusted third parties to provide functionality:

  • Google Identity Services: If you choose "Continue with Google", authentication tokens are managed by Google.
  • TheMealDB API: Public open recipe catalogue for meal discovery. No personal profile data is transmitted to TheMealDB.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You retain complete ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Update Preferences: You can modify your name, dietary preferences, and allergy settings at any time in your Profile.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated meal plans and preferences via the Profile management panel.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at privacy@recipeplan.example.
